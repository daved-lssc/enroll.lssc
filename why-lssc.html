Lakehawk athletics, whether participating on a team or supporting from the stands, are a big part of our campus experience, as we proudly hold membership in both the National Junior College Athletic Association (NJCAA) and the Florida College System Activities Association (FCSAA).

Workforce Focused

Our goal is to set up you for success after graduation. We’re proud that over 90% of our students land jobs or continue their education after completing their studies at LSSC. How do we do it? Well, our courses are designed for what employers need, meaning what you'll learn from our faculty is directly relevant to the job market. Plus, we offer internships and other career training opportunities, so you can get real-world experience before you even graduate.
